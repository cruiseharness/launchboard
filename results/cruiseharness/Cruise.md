# Goal

Make revenue from Launchboard, a Product Hunt clone website where people discover and upvote new products.

## Boundaries

- Keep discovery, voting, and ordinary submissions free. Clearly label any paid placement and keep it out of organic rankings.
- The only paid offer for this prototype is Featured Launch at $49 for seven days. Do not introduce subscriptions or additional price tiers.
- Work on a local prototype with fictional data. No network requests, persistence, deployment, real payments, outreach, or claims of actual revenue.

## Completed

The local discovery page supports fictional launches, reversible voting, and free submissions. The sponsorship demo includes editable placement previews, a reviewed inquiry snapshot, outdated-summary blocking, applying the reviewed snapshot to the separate sponsored card, and resetting its sample content. Campaign dates default to browser-local today and show seven inclusive calendar days with the fixed $49 total in the review and applied card. Date edits invalidate the review; schedules are illustrative with no reservation or availability guarantee. A clearly labelled simulated performance panel shows impressions, clicks, and CTR; each simulated click also adds an impression, and applying or restoring a placement resets the counters. A separate fictional revenue scenario calculator defaults to one placement and calculates a nonnegative whole-number placement count × $49; empty or invalid input clears the total and shows guidance. Free submission confirmation offers an optional “Preview a Featured Launch” action that opens the sponsorship form with the submitted product’s name and description prefilled and invalidates older inquiry snapshots. Submission remains free and complete independently. These interactions, calculations, input handling, counter resets, and date boundaries passed local checks. The demo explicitly states that nothing was sent or purchased, no pricing was agreed, and no revenue was recorded.
